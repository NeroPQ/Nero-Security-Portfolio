Dirty PipeCVE-2022-0847

Validated two privilege-escalation approaches against a vulnerable Linux kernel in a disposable training VM.

CVSS BASE SCORE7.8
RESEARCH AREALocal Privilege Escalation
LAB OUTCOMERoot privileges
01 / RESEARCH

Investigation approach

Researched pipe buffers, page-cache overwrites and the PIPE_BUF_FLAG_CAN_MERGE condition; exercised a controlled passwd-file overwrite and a SUID binary technique.

02 / ROOT CAUSE

What made it possible

Improper initialization of pipe_buffer flags permits writes into file-backed cached pages despite ordinary read-only file permissions.

03 / EVIDENCE

What the lab demonstrated

  • PoC-based /etc/passwd overwrite
  • su and id confirmed UID 0
  • SUID binary technique confirmed UID 0
  • Cleanup screenshot for temporary SUID helper
04 / SCOPE

Limitations

Kernel build was not captured and complete cleanup of the passwd-file technique was not independently verified.

Original research files

Source material and screenshots are preserved in the public GitHub repository.
